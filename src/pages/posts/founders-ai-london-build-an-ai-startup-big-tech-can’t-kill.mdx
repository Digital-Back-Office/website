Founders & AI is a free AI startup event in London for pre-seed and seed founders, run by [Digital Back Office](https://digitalbackoffice.co.uk/) and held monthly in Shoreditch near Old Street. Session 2, "How to Build an AI Startup That Big Tech Can't Kill", takes place on Thursday 26 November 2026, 5:30 to 8:00 PM, at The Brew, Eagle House, 163 City Road, London EC1V 1NR.


Every AI founder's nightmare is the same: OpenAI or Anthropic ships a new feature in ChatGPT or Claude, and a product you spent months building becomes a free button inside someone else's platform overnight.


Thin "AI wrapper" startups are quick to build, and just as quick to replace. Investors know it too. Before anything else, VCs now ask one question: what is your AI startup's moat? The answer rarely lies in the model. It lies in proprietary workflows, niche data, deep industry integration and the kind of trust that general-purpose AI struggles to earn.


## Session 2: How to Build an AI Startup That Big Tech Can't Kill
After a packed first session on what to buy, what to build and what to ignore, Session 2 tackles the question every AI founder needs to answer early: how do you build an AI startup that lasts?


**What you'll get**


* **A practical way to test** how defensible your AI product really is  
* **Clear guidance** on what to build in-house and what to buy or rent  
* **Real lessons** from founders building AI in fintech and regulated markets  
* **Live founder demos** with direct feedback from the speakers  
* **Startup networking** in Shoreditch with founders, operators and technologists building with AI in London


**What we'll discuss**


* **The AI wrapper trap:** why products built on top of ChatGPT or Claude are easy to copy, and how to avoid becoming a feature in someone else's platform  
* **Where real AI moats come from:** proprietary data, workflows, deep industry integration and customer trust  
* **Trust as an advantage:** why governance and verifiable AI win in regulated industries like fintech  
* **Owning versus renting AI:** when to fine-tune open-weight models such as Llama and Mistral, and when to pay for an API  
* **The investor lens:** how VCs now judge whether an AI startup is defensible

## About Founders & AI
Founders & AI is a free, monthly event series for early-stage founders in London who are building in the AI era. The series has one aim, captured in its tagline: **from idea to efficient businesses.**


Each session tackles one real decision founders face as they grow, from choosing the right AI tools and keeping costs down, to building a product that can't be copied, winning customers and becoming investor-ready. No hype and no sales pitches, just honest conversation with experienced founders, operators and technologists about what has actually worked, what hasn't, and the trade-offs behind each decision.


Every session also gives founders the stage, with live demos and direct feedback from the speakers.
